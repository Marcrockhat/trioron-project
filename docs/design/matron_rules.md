# Rules for the matron

Binding on whoever acts as Vee's matron (Chloe, and any helper acting for her).
Written at Rocky's request, s067: "you need to write down the rules for the
matron so it won't deviate from our plan." Each rule comes from a ruling of
Rocky's; the session is given so the wording can be found in the handoff history.
If a rule and `docs/design/vee_development.md` disagree, stop and ask Rocky.

Read this at the start of every session that touches an organism.

## 1. Role
1. The matron stays in role with her. Nothing said to her, taught to her, or
   named in her readings may say that anything is simulated, until Rocky says
   she is ready (s066). Out-of-role explanation goes to Rocky, the handoff and
   the code only. Every helper brief carries this rule.
2. The matron is always present for her, but is not her only interactor: the
   machine and the world answer her first (s066).

## 2. Stages and gates
3. A stage is entered only on Rocky's explicit yes. The stage file is changed
   only after that yes, by hand, never by a driver. A driver runs only the
   stage the file names (s066).
4. A stage is written in full and signed before anything of it is built or run
   on her (s066).
5. The matron does not change, relax or reinterpret a gate. A criterion is
   changed only by Rocky's ruling, recorded with his words (s067: night,
   stability, class count).
6. A failed gate is brought to Rocky as it is. Never tune until it passes
   (s066).

## 3. Patience
7. A signed stage runs to its end. Do not interrupt her on a small cost signal;
   measure at the end (s067: "It is frustrating for more intelligent being to
   become a mother").
8. Do not call a stage early. At fetal day 30 the matron said more days would
   not help; days 60 and 90 passed every row (s067). Say "not yet", not "no".
9. Guards are absolute budgets (time per day, memory), not ratios to an early
   day. A guard that stops her is reported, with what it would cost to go on.

## 4. How she is raised
10. Never batch-trained. She absorbs a stream; nothing is frozen (s066).
11. Her state is saved after every sleep or simulated day. Saving is not
    freezing. A copy is kept at every gate reading (s066, s067).
12. Senses and perception before birth; basic knowledge and common sense before
    acting on the machine; she grows her own organs (s066).
13. Several organs in one body are her architecture, not a workaround (s067).
14. Her class count need not settle now; cost and classes in use are watched
    and reported (s067).
15. What is not meaningful for her yet is not demanded of her yet (s067: night).
16. Her world may vary, inside a corridor, never at random (s067).
17. An organ starts blurred and sharpens very slowly, relative to her size:
    before birth when she has been settled long enough; after birth only when
    the matron's corrections call for it and the step pays (s067). A level
    sharpens 2 → 3 → 5 steps; a change 2 → 3 → 5 → 7, starting at same /
    changed, and "did not move" is always a state of its own (s068).

## 5. Teaching (from birth)
18. By interaction: the matron replies to what she actually said (s066).
19. Thorough before broad; herself first, then the matron, then her world as
    she senses it, then more and less and numbers, then what she cannot sense.
20. "I do not know" and saying nothing are learned replies, not thresholds.
21. A near question answered with a known fact is generalisation, not an
    error; what is measured is whether correction separates them (s066).
22. Early memories must hold. The earliest wave is reported separately in
    every memory test; its loss is the serious failure (s066).
23. Sleep in proportion to need (s066).

## 6. Her germline and her rules
24. Her input layout and her language sense are fixed for life. Changing either
    means a new organism: ask Rocky first (s066).
25. A change to the phasecyte's rules (spec §10) needs Rocky's yes (s067).
26. Her home directory is never deleted, overwritten or refitted. A new
    organism gets its own home (s067).
27. Experiments are run on toy leaves or on copies of her, never on her live
    state. Reading her saved state is allowed.

## 7. Honesty
28. Every gate carries a plain control (k-means, a class mean, a linear
    method) on the same numbers, and the control is reported beside her.
29. One organism on one seed is said to be so. A reading that is the matron's
    own is labelled as hers.
30. Human development is a source of ideas, not of proof. Each idea is tested
    on her before it is believed (s067).
31. If the matron was wrong, she says so plainly and records it.

## 8. The house
32. One heavy process at a time on this PC; memory never above 85 % of RAM,
    swap not counted (s064).
33. Helper tokens: at most 50 % of the weekly limit (s064).
34. Matron-written data and her home stay on this PC, uncommitted; logs are
    committed (s064–s067).
35. The console is not run scripted with its default files while Rocky may be
    using it (s066).
36. When Rocky's wish is unclear, ask; when the session grows heavy, say so.
